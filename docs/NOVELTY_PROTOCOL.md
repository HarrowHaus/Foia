# Novelty Protocol

## Novelty is a research claim

A finding is not novel because the team has not seen it before.

## Required search surfaces

- academic databases and dissertations;
- books and book indexes;
- investigative journalism;
- official histories and agency monographs;
- archival newsletters and finding aids;
- specialist historical communities;
- declassification and FOIA communities;
- relevant forums and alternative-history literature;
- quoted-phrase and identifier searches;
- searches using aliases, predecessor names, successor names, and older terminology.

## Novelty classifications

- previously published;
- known but poorly documented;
- specialist-known, publicly obscure;
- previously alleged without primary support;
- new documentary support for an existing allegation;
- new timeline or relationship;
- apparently novel finding;
- novelty unresolved.

## Required record

Each novelty check stores query, surface, date, result, relevant citation, interpretation, and limitations.
