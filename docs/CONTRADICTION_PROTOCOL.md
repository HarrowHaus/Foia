# Contradiction Protocol

## Types

- public statement versus internal record;
- two official timelines;
- role or employment-date conflict;
- funding or program-status conflict;
- duplicate document with materially different text;
- later recollection versus contemporaneous record;
- classification/declassification marking inconsistency;
- source citation that does not support the citing claim.

## Procedure

1. State each proposition separately.
2. Identify whether both propositions can be true under a narrower interpretation.
3. Compare document dates, authorship, purpose, audience, version, and chain of custody.
4. Search for amendments, errata, later releases, and successor terminology.
5. Record ordinary explanations before concealment explanations.
6. Leave the contradiction open when evidence is insufficient.

A contradiction is a research object, not proof of a predetermined theory.
